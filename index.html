The problem

Moving to university is a big change.

College and university do not work in exactly the same way. At university, students must manage more responsibilities, communicate with new people, follow new rules, and study more independently. UniBridge helps students take this transition step by step.

    About the project

    How UniBridge connects with sociology.

    The prototype applies two core ideas from sociology: socialization and resocialization.

    Concept 1

    Socialization

    Socialization is the process of learning the rules, behavior, and expectations of a group. In this project, students learn how university life works.

    Concept 2

    Resocialization

    Resocialization is the process of replacing old habits with new habits when entering a new environment. In this project, students replace college habits with university habits.

    Concept 3

    Anticipatory Socialization

    This happens before joining university. Students learn about university rules, campus locations, communication, and study expectations.

    Concept 4

    Resocialization Support

    This happens after joining university. Students receive reminders, mentor support, first-week activities, and study-planning tools.
